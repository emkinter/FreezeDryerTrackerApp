# FreezeDryerTracker 1.1

Follow active batches at a glance, log each stage to the second, and keep a portable copy of your records.

## Follow your runs

- The **Home** screen shows active and recently completed runs. When there is enough history, it estimates the drying finish from completed runs on the same freeze dryer.
- The **Run Status** widget opens on the newest active batch. Browse other active runs, see an all-runs summary, and check the stage, elapsed time, Time Log, and estimated drying finish.
- The **Apple Watch** companion brings the same run overview to your wrist. Switch between runs and log stages using **Exact Time** or **Time Ago** while connected to your paired iPhone.

## Record each stage and tray

- The **Time Log** groups recorded stages by date and shows seconds. New entries suggest the next stage, accept an exact date and time or an hours, minutes, and seconds offset, and can use a photo of the freeze dryer panel to help fill in the time.
- Run summaries show prep, drying, extra drying, and defrost durations. You can log when defrost starts and see seconds in the completed run total.
- Choose metric, imperial, or both in Settings. Add weights together in the empty and loaded tray fields, then see net loaded weight, net dried weight, and water removed. Existing weight entries are kept in grams.
- Tray navigation stays visible while the keyboard is open. You can record Harvest Right machine settings and let the app generate dryer and run names when you leave the name blank.

## Manage your data

- Choose local storage, personal iCloud sync, or a shared family dataset. Sync recovery handles interrupted transfers, and owners can manage duplicate datasets or replace their shared iCloud data with the device copy.
- **Settings → Data & Backups** exports JSON backups, imports missing records, and restores a backup over the open dataset. New exports go into the **FreezeDryerTracker** folder in Files on iPhone and iPad. On Mac, choose **Show Backup Folder in Finder**.
- **Reset All Data** erases app records and recovery backups from the device and removes personal iCloud records after confirmation. Only the person who created a family dataset can erase it for everyone.
