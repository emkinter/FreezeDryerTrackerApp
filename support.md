# FreezeDryerTracker Support

FreezeDryerTracker helps you log freeze dryers, runs, individual trays, maintenance, and food inventory on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Contact support

Email [emkinter@me.com](mailto:emkinter@me.com) for help, bug reports, or suggestions.

Include your device model, operating system version, app version, what you expected, and what happened. If an error appears, include its exact text or a screenshot with private details hidden. For a sync issue, say whether you are using a personal or family dataset.

Never send your Apple Account password. Share only the records or attachments you are comfortable providing; an entire backup is usually unnecessary for an initial report.

## Getting started

Add a freeze dryer and enter its manufacturer, model, size, and default empty-tray weight. Create a run for that dryer, then record each tray's contents and weights. The default tray weight is used to fill new trays for that dryer. You can update trays individually and navigate to the next tray while recording a batch.

Use maintenance records for servicing and inventory for packaged food and storage details. Choose your weight unit and default electricity rate in Settings.

## How do I sync my own devices?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled on each device. Make sure you have an internet connection and enough available iCloud storage, then open FreezeDryerTracker and allow time for synchronization. Check the iCloud status shown in Settings.

If records appear different, first confirm that both devices are using the same personal or family dataset. Personal records and a family dataset are separate; they do not continually copy into each other.

You can save changes while offline. Keep the app open after reconnecting so pending changes have time to sync. Before reinstalling or troubleshooting with deletion, export a backup of any records that have not appeared on your other devices.

## How do I share with my family?

1. Open **Settings → Family Sharing** and choose **Create Family Dataset**. This copies your personal records into a separate family dataset and keeps your personal records intact.
2. Choose **Manage Sharing** and invite the intended people using Apple's sharing controls.
3. Recipients open the iCloud invitation and join in FreezeDryerTracker. If needed, paste the invitation into **iCloud Invitation Link** and choose **Open Invitation**.
4. On another device for an account that already owns or has joined the dataset, use **Find Family Datasets** to locate it.

Every participant can view and update the whole family dataset, including future records. Membership in Apple's Family Sharing purchase group alone does not grant access; an invitation is required.

Use **Sync Now** to request a family update. Changes to different records, fields, and trays merge. If two people change the same field, the next successful sync wins; a concurrent deletion wins over an edit. Coordinate edits to the same record when possible.

## How do I stop sharing or return to my personal records?

The owner uses **Manage Sharing** to remove participants or stop sharing. Participants can use Apple's sharing controls to leave.

**Open Personal Dataset** switches your current view; it does not stop sharing or revoke anyone's access. Existing family caches and exported copies can remain on participants' devices. If access is removed, export a backup of any needed local records before removing the app. Ask the owner for a new invitation if you should still have access.

## How do I back up or restore records?

In Settings, choose **Export Backup…** and save the file somewhere you can find later. The export contains the currently open dataset, so export personal and family datasets separately if you need both. Store backups securely because they may contain serial numbers, notes, and other details you entered.

Use **Restore from Backup…** to select a saved file. Restoring replaces the current dataset and can synchronize those changes to other devices or family members. Export the current dataset before restoring so you have a copy of it.

Deleting records also synchronizes to the other devices using that dataset. Removing the app does not necessarily remove data in iCloud or exported backups.

## Does this app control my freeze dryer or certify food safety?

FreezeDryerTracker records information you enter; it does not control or automatically monitor the machine. Electricity costs, shelf life, and best-by dates are estimates based on your inputs. Follow your equipment manufacturer's instructions and appropriate food-safety guidance when deciding whether food is fully dried and safe to store.

Manufacturer links open product pages in your default browser. For equipment repairs or product questions, contact the manufacturer directly.
