# FreezeDryerTracker Privacy Policy

> **Draft for the app owner — not legal advice.** Before publishing, verify that this policy accurately describes the app, any third-party SDKs, and your support practices. Have a legal professional review it. Remove this draft notice only after completing that review.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

FreezeDryerTracker is developed by Ekkehard Koch. This policy describes how information is handled when you use FreezeDryerTracker on iPhone, iPad, or Mac, use its iCloud features, or contact support.

## Information you enter

The app stores the records and settings you choose to enter. These may include:

- Freeze dryer names, manufacturers, models, sizes, serial numbers, purchase dates, pump hours, and default tray weights.
- Run and batch details, tray contents and weights, preparation details, timestamps, notes, error notes, and quality ratings.
- Maintenance records, inventory quantities, storage locations, packaging details, and estimated shelf life.
- Preferences such as weight units and electricity rates used for estimates.

These records support the app's logging, calculations, inventory, backup, and synchronization features. The app does not automatically read information from or control your freeze dryer. Avoid putting sensitive personal information in names or free-text notes unless you intend to store and, where applicable, share it.

## Storage and personal iCloud synchronization

The app saves records locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. It can be used locally without creating a separate app account.

When iCloud is available, personal records and supported preferences synchronize through Apple's CloudKit service under your Apple Account. This lets you access them on your other devices signed in to the same account. Local changes can be saved offline and synchronized when iCloud becomes available.

CloudKit account and record identifiers are used to locate data, manage access to shared datasets, and keep local caches associated with the appropriate account. The app does not ask for or receive your Apple Account password. There is no separate developer-operated server receiving your app records.

Apple operates iCloud and handles information under its own terms and [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Cloud storage and availability depend on your Apple Account, settings, and Apple's service.

## Optional family dataset sharing

Family sharing is optional and uses Apple's CloudKit invitations. It is not enabled automatically by belonging to an Apple Family Sharing purchase group.

Creating a family dataset copies your current personal dataset into a separate shared dataset. The original personal dataset remains separate. People who accept an invitation can view and edit the entire shared dataset, including freeze dryers, runs, trays, maintenance, inventory, preferences, and records added later. Sharing is not limited to selected records or read-only access.

The owner can manage participants or stop sharing through **Manage Sharing**. Opening your personal dataset does not stop sharing or remove anyone's access. Removing access does not retract copies, exported backups, or cached information that participants already have. Local family caches may remain on devices after access ends.

## Backups and files you choose to share

**Export Backup…** saves a file containing the currently open dataset to a location you choose. If you select a cloud drive or share that file, the destination service or recipient receives it. Exported backups are separate from the app's iCloud synchronization and remain wherever you save or share them until you remove them.

**Restore from Backup…** replaces the currently open dataset. Changes to an iCloud-synchronized or family dataset may then propagate to other devices or participants. Protect backup files and share them only with intended recipients.

## Support messages

If you email support, the information you provide may include your email address, message, device or app details, and attachments. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Your email provider and the support mailbox provider process those communications.

Please send only what is needed to describe the problem. Do not send your Apple Account password or a complete dataset or backup unless you choose to provide it for troubleshooting. Contact support about access to or deletion of information you have sent by email.

## Advertising, analytics, and external websites

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, third-party crash-reporting SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell app records or use them for advertising. The app does not request access to contacts, location, photos, microphone, or health data. Apple may separately process service and diagnostic information according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

Optional manufacturer links, including Harvest Right product links, open external websites in your default browser. Those sites apply their own privacy policies. If these support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process visitor information under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The app does not upload your dataset to GitHub.

## Retention and your choices

You can edit or delete records in the app. Deletions from a synchronized dataset propagate through iCloud when synchronization succeeds. Personal datasets, family datasets, local caches, and exported backups are separate copies; removing information from one does not automatically erase all the others.

Deleting the app alone does not guarantee removal of iCloud records, other devices' copies, support emails, or exported files. You can also review your iCloud storage and account controls in Apple's settings. Retention in Apple's services and backups is subject to Apple's controls and policies; this app does not promise immediate permanent deletion from every backup or recipient's device.

For questions or requests about information provided to support, use the contact below. The developer cannot access your private iCloud records simply because you contact support.
